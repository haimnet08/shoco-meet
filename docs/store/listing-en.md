# Shoco Meet – Store listing text (English)

**Name:** Shoco Meet
**Short description:** Video meetings and remote support, no account, no complicated setup.

**Description:**
Shoco Meet is a lightweight video-meeting app by Shoco Marketing. Open a room, send the link, and the other side joins from the browser or from the app.
The desktop app adds remote support: automatic screen sharing, and when the supporter asks to control your mouse and keyboard you approve it in a dialog on your own computer and can stop at any time by closing the app.

Features:
- Video and audio directly between participants (encrypted WebRTC)
- Screen sharing, chat and local recording
- Remote control only after explicit consent
- Invite links that open the app straight into the right room
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking

**Search terms:** video meeting, remote control, remote support, screen sharing
**What's new:** First Store release.
